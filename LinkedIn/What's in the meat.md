Why are we still talking to bots one at a time?

Humans don’t innovate alone.
They collaborate.

Two people brainstorm.
Five people shape strategy.
Ten people build companies.

But in AI?

One human.
One bot.
Session closed.

Some platforms let you invite another humans —
but they keep humans and bots in separate lanes.

They build silos.
I’m building a bridge.

In the system I’m building, we don’t discriminate.

Start with a human.
Invite a specialized bot.
Add another human for oversight.
Drop in a second bot for heavy data lifting.

That’s how conversations scale.

Intelligence is intelligence — regardless of the substrate.

To paraphrase Minsky:
“What’s so special about the meat?”

If you’re building for the future, your participant list shouldn’t care about your pulse — only your contribution.
