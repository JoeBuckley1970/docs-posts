**Will AI cause a “Waterfall Model” comeback?**

Full disclaimer: I use AI. A lot. And when I say a lot — I mean a lot!

Before AI, I would often start coding without writing much down. The design was in my head. Very Agile.

That way of working doesn’t translate well to AI.

These days, I find myself spending a few hours — sometimes even a full day — planning what I want to achieve and writing it down clearly.

Then I translate that thinking into prompts and structured requests.

It feels a bit like Waterfall — but fast and small. More like a rapid than a waterfall.

So, I’m officially coining the phrase:

**“Rapid Model for AI Development.”**

Upfront clarity. Fast execution. Tight feedback loops.

I’ll leave the full process definition to you.
